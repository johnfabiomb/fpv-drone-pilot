About Explore Malta

Created by FPV drone pilot and content creator John Montaño.

Explore Malta is an original travel resource for Malta and Gozo. It was built to help visitors discover hidden caves, coastal viewpoints, valley trails and off-the-beaten-path locations with carefully curated content and an interactive map.

The site is owned and maintained by John Montaño, who owns the source code, images, and location research published here.

What you will find here

  • More than 60 unique Malta and Gozo locations.
  • Original guides, photos and route suggestions.
  • Fast mobile-friendly navigation and map tools.
  • Privacy and cookie policies for visitor transparency.

Why this site exists

This site was created to share real travel inspiration and local exploration tips, not to serve only as an ad destination. It aims to provide useful, authentic content for people who want to explore Malta’s natural and cultural highlights.
