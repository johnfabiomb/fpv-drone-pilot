Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by John Montaño (johnfabiomb.com), an FPV drone pilot and content creator based in Malta. You can contact us at johnfabiomb@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and device information, collected automatically via Google Analytics.
  • Location data — only if you explicitly grant permission through your browser. This is used solely to show your position on the map and find nearby spots. It is never stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Cookies and tracking data — see our Cookie Policy for full details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the interactive map and location guides.
  • To understand how visitors use the site (Google Analytics).
  • To serve relevant advertisements (Google AdSense).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about site usage. Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visitor behaviour. Data collected is processed by Google Inc. and may be transferred to servers in the United States. We have enabled IP anonymisation. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For EEA visitors, we use Google's Funding Choices consent management platform to obtain your consent before serving personalised ads.

6. Payments

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not collect, store, or process any payment card data. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers where necessary to operate the site:

  • Google LLC (Analytics, AdSense, Fonts, Maps)
  • Stripe Inc. (Payment processing)

8. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for 26 months as configured in Google Analytics. We do not store personal data on our own servers.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data where there is no legitimate reason to continue processing it.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at johnfabiomb@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (Malta).

10. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
