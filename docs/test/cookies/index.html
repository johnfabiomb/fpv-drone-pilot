What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit and can improve your experience on return visits.

Managing Your Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Consent banner — When you first visit the site, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner.
  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Refer to your browser's help documentation: Chrome, Firefox, Safari.
  • Google Ads opt-out — Opt out of personalised ads at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Industry opt-out — youronlinechoices.eu (EU) or aboutads.info.

Note: disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site.
