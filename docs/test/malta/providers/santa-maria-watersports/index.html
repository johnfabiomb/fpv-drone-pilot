Private wakeboard & wakesurf boat charter in Malta

Santa Maria Water Sports runs private boat charters from the Valletta Waterfront aboard a Nautique G25 — one of the world's finest performance wake boats. Whether you want to learn wakeboard, ride wakesurf, fly on a wakefoil, or simply cruise Malta's dramatic coastline, their certified instructors tailor every session to your level. Charters run from 1 hour to a full day and can reach any point along Malta's coast from the Grand Harbour.
