Self-drive Quad Bike Tour — Gozo & Comino

Ride your own 570cc quad through Gozo's wild backroads

Driving in convoy behind a skilled tour leader, power through rugged valleys and backroads on a punchy 570cc quad bike. You'll visit Gozo's sleepy villages, wild countryside and epic coast — Yippee's multi-award-winning tours unlock secluded spots and neighbourhoods only the locals know.

The day opens with a boat trip to Comino: swim and snorkel the Blue Lagoon and Crystal Lagoon and slip into hidden caves (swim stop June–October) before the quad adventure across Gozo.

~7 hours (excl. transfers) · all-inclusive · all year round.

  • €115 driver only · €195 driver + passenger
  • Drivers must be 21+, fully licensed — provisional licences not accepted
  • Includes: hotel pickup & drop-off, boat crossing to Gozo, light picnic lunch, English/French-speaking tour leader, helmets, raincoats, fuel & insurance

Stops include: Ta' Ċenċ Cliffs, Xlendi, Dwejra, Ta' Pinu, Wied il-Għasri, Xwejni Salt Pans, Marsalforn, Blue Lagoon & Comino's caves. Also available as a seasonal Sunset Tour (mid-April–mid-October).
