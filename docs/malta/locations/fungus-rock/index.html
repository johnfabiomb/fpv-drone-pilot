Fungus Rock
Rock FormationLandmarkViewpointNatureGozoEasy 4.6
Fungus Rock is one of Gozo’s most iconic natural landmarks, rising dramatically from the sea at the entrance of Dwejra Bay. Known locally as 'Il-Ġebla tal-Ġeneral,' this massive limestone islet has a rich history and was once guarded for its rare medicinal fungus. Today, it remains a symbol of raw coastal beauty, surrounded by deep blue waters and towering cliffs. It’s a breathtaking viewpoint for hikers, photographers, and anyone seeking a front-row seat to Gozo’s wild side.
